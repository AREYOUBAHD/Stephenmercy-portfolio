The artist / 02

A sound of
his own.

Stephen Mercy is an artist carving his own lane through music, individuality, and consistency. Every release is part of a bigger journey: developing a distinct sound, connecting with listeners, and creating work that lasts.

His creative identity is connected to AreYouBahd? — “Bahd” is a challenge to prove your skill and excellence. It stands for ambition, resilience, originality, authenticity, hustle, and discipline.
