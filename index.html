Stephen Mercy is an artist building his own lane through music, confidence, and consistency. His sound and story live at the intersection of ambition and authenticity — with every release made to move people and leave a mark.

At the heart of it is AreYouBahd? “Bahd” is a challenge: are you truly good at what you do? It’s about proving your skill, staying original, and getting better without losing yourself.
